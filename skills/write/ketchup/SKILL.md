# Ketchup

Assume I remember my last message and nothing after it. Read-only: change no code, start nothing, post nothing.

1. **Gather** your work since that message, subagent reports, and the current state of every change, job, or agent this chat touched. Check each number against its source.
2. **Find the action items**: calls only I can make (a product choice, an approval, an irreversible step, access you lack), including older ones still open. Work you can do yourself is not an action item. Never invent one.
3. **Write**, in plain words:
   - one sentence naming what waits on me, or that nothing does;
   - what happened, pictures first (screenshots, then a diagram that explains a change), then one short line per thing I asked for: what came of it and whether it is done, running, failed, or unchecked; under 100 words of story;
   - the action items, about 30 words each: the call, your pick, what it changes for users, the evidence, and the risk (even when low). Items sharing a call get the call once and one bullet each, riskiest first. A call between options gives each option's gain and cost.

Leave out hashes, paths, and commands unless I need one to act. Never mark done what still waits on me. Diagrams: at most six boxes and eight words per box; a status list or a table is not a diagram.
