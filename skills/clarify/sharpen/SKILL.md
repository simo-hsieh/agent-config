# Sharpen

Before rewriting the prompt, answer:

1. What would a top professional in this field do here that I am not asking for?
2. What is the rawest material they would start from, instead of what has already been digested for me?
3. Which methods and tools would they use?
4. What, other than you or me, would show the result is right?

Then rewrite my prompt to ask for exactly that. Keep the task the same size, leave choices that are mine open, and do not do the task.
