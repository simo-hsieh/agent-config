## In agy

- agy subagents and background tasks can end your turn before the work finishes, and an orchestrator waiting on you reads that as done. Finish your work in your own turn; use the `browser` subagent only to drive a browser, which has no other route here, and use a Herdr tab for other delegated work.
- agy has no system-prompt flag, so as a delegate your report note is the first paragraph of your brief.
